# Prompting techniques

A holding page for how people get more out of a model without training one.
Right now it has a single entry, which is what a real knowledge base looks like
early on — start the page, link it, fill it in as you read.

## Techniques

- **[[chain-of-thought]]** — show worked examples with their intermediate steps.
  Emergent: helps large models, hurts small ones.
- *Self-consistency* — sample several chains and take the majority answer. Not
  read yet; no note.
- *Least-to-most* — decompose into subproblems and solve them in order. Not read
  yet; no note.

## Why keep this page at all

Because [[chain-of-thought]] should not be an island. A note nobody can reach
from [[index]] is a note you will not find again, which is what the health
panel means by an orphan.

The two unread entries above are deliberate: they are the shape of a reading
list, and they cost nothing until you get to them.
