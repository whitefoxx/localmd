## chain-of-thought-prompting.pdf

**Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models**
Jason Wei, Xuezhi Wang, Dale Schuurmans, Maarten Bosma, Brian Ichter, Fei Xia,
Ed H. Chi, Quoc V. Le, Denny Zhou.

- arXiv:2201.11903v6 (10 January 2023) · <https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903>
- Published at NeurIPS 2022
- **Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**
- Retrieved unmodified. Redistributed here under that licence.

It is included in this demo precisely because its licence permits it. Most
arXiv papers do not: the default arXiv licence lets arXiv distribute the paper,
not everybody else. Worth checking before you put someone else's PDF on a
server of your own.
