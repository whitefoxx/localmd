# Trademark

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the code. It does not cover the name
**localmd** or the localmd logo, which identify the hosted service at
<https://localmd.app>.

Copyright licences and trademarks are different things, and MIT is silent on
the second. This note says out loud what that silence means, so nobody has to
guess:

- **Fork it, build it, deploy it, sell what you make with it.** That is what the
  MIT licence says and it is meant.
- **Please give your copy its own name.** Not because forks are unwelcome, but
  because a second thing called localmd, on an address that is not localmd.app,
  makes it impossible for anyone to tell whose bug they are reporting or whose
  build they are running.
- Saying what your project is *built on* is fine and always was — "based on
  localmd", "a fork of localmd". What the name should not do is stand in for
  yours.

This lives in its own file rather than at the bottom of LICENSE because a
licence file with anything appended to it stops being recognisable to the tools
that read licences, and a repository whose licence shows as "unknown" is a
worse outcome for everyone than a slightly longer file list.
